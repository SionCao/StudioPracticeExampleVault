## Session focus
This session was about how to offer and receive feedback in studio practice. We learned about the DasArts Feedback Method, including affirmative feedback, perspectives, and open questions. The main focus was understanding how feedback can help us develop our work, rather than simply finding problems or judging whether something is good or bad.
## Inquiry
I wanted to understand how to listen to different opinions and use them to improve my work. Sometimes, as a maker, I become too familiar with my own ideas and may overlook things that other people can easily notice. I wanted to explore how feedback could help me see beyond my own perspective.
## Feedback
One important thing I learned is that everyone looks at an artwork differently. People have different experiences, knowledge, and interests, so they notice different things. This can bring new questions and possibilities to a project, including things the artist may not have considered. I also found the idea of sharing perspectives through "As... I need..." interesting. It reminds us that feedback comes from a personal point of view, rather than an absolute truth. Starting with "What worked for me" and asking open questions can also make feedback more useful and respectful.
## Next steps
In future feedback sessions, I want to listen more carefully before explaining or defending my choices. I will try to ask clearer questions about my work and take notes on different perspectives. I do not need to follow every suggestion, but I can use feedback to discover things I have overlooked and make my work clearer and more complete.
